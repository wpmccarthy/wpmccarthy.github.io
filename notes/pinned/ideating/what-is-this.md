This is a collection of writing exploring how the practices of art and design are evolving alongside the rise of AI.

The loftier vision is that it might steer the development of AI in a direction that is more beneficial for artists and designers.

More personally, I’m writing this to refine my thinking on the relationship between technology and art, to ensure that I’m not ceding my thinking to the machines, and to reach people who also care about this.

Rather than a chronological feed of posts, this will be a set of documents that evolve as the landscape and my thinking on the topics changes.
